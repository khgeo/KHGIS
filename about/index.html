What KHGIS adds

Khmer interface

1,255 translated strings. Switch the whole app between English and ភាសាខ្មែរ; anything untranslated falls back to English.

Khmer that renders right

Noto Sans Khmer is bundled and the renderer shapes Khmer properly, so labels, legends and exported maps stay readable.

Cambodia built in

Province (ADM1) and district (ADM2) boundaries ship with the app. No download, no projection fiddling before you start.

One-click area loading

Name a province and get its boundary plus roads, rivers, schools, health facilities and villages from OpenStreetMap, clipped to the area.

A4 maps in one step

Title, legend, scale bar, north arrow, regional inset, sources and date — print-ready, in Khmer or English.

Cambodia-aware assistant

Understands province names in Khmer, English and loose romanisations, and replies in Khmer when you write in Khmer.

Workflows

CommandWhat it answers
floodExposureVillages within a distance of rivers and water
schoolAccessVillages farther than a distance from any school
healthAccessVillages farther than a distance from any health facility
districtSchoolsSchools counted per district, as a choropleth

Every action is a command

rustgis-cli run \
  --cmd 'project.cambodia={"khmer":true}' \
  --cmd 'khgis.loadArea={"province":"Kandal","themes":["water","places"],"lang":"km"}' \
  --cmd 'khgis.recipe={"id":"floodExposure","distance":3,"lang":"km"}' \
  --cmd 'khgis.autoLayout={"lang":"km"}' \
  --save kandal.rgis
